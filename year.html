CADIZ RADIO All papers

Harrison County, Ohio · 21 March – 18 September 1844

The year in the type

This is a first-person paper, not a textbook. The Sentinel prints every week from the same office. The masthead stays. The date changes. Shops take stands and keep taking them. National news arrives when the exchanges send it. Nothing here was rewritten. Names are as printed. Cadiz is spoken KAY-diz.

Six Thursdays are recorded. Twenty-one later numbers are still type only. Walk a thread, or a door on the square, and the player opens on that clip.
