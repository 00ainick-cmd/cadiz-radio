# History and genealogy repos

This is the map. Do not start a fifth app.

## Living

| Repo | What it is | Do |
|---|---|---|
| [Lineage](https://github.com/00ainick-cmd/Lineage) | The interactive history app. Trees, place microhistories (Cadiz and other towns), Wayside locations. Data lives in the browser (IndexedDB). If it is not in Lineage, it did not happen as research. | Build here. |
| [cadiz-radio](https://github.com/00ainick-cmd/cadiz-radio) | The voice. Sentinel issues you can hear. A view that feeds Lineage. | Keep. Do not dump the tree in here. |
| [wayside](https://github.com/00ainick-cmd/wayside) | The school-science book. Georgetown first. Cadiz stays locked until Nick opens it. | Keep. Do not unlock Cadiz from this file. |

Live files on disk (not a substitute for Lineage): OneDrive `Ancestry` and Genesis `50-Genealogy`. Specs Nick reviews stay in Genesis. Research claims get filed in Lineage.

## Law

The printed source is the witness. Lineage is where the claim is filed. Radio and the walk are views. Wayside is a later door. No invented rooms, people, or plats. Cadiz is a town file, not a Brown hunt.
